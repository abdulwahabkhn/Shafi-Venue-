# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React with Vite and TypeScript, chosen for a fast, maintainable, responsive public venue website with a client-side booking enquiry experience.

## Users

- People in and around Jaranwala evaluating a venue for a wedding or other hosted event.
- Families and event decision-makers who need to see the venue, understand what can be arranged, check their date, and start a booking conversation.
- Shafi Complex & Marquee staff who will receive and follow up on booking enquiries.

## Product Purpose

Present Shafi Complex & Marquee clearly and credibly, show authentic venue and stage imagery, and turn interest into a structured booking enquiry that staff can confirm directly.

## Positioning

The publicly verified differentiator is customizable event staging: the venue describes elegant, royal, simple, and grand stage directions that can be tailored to the customer's choice. Broader competitive claims remain unconfirmed.

## Operating Context

- Public venue discovery and booking-enquiry journey, primarily on mobile and also on desktop and tablet.
- Verified venue: Shafi Complex & Marquee, 1-Km on Shehruana Road, Kamuana Bypass, Jaranwala, Pakistan.
- Verified contact: 0313 8220777 and Scjaranwala@hotmail.com.
- Verified public channels: Facebook page “Shafi complex & marquee” and TikTok account `@shafi_complex_marquee`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed business categories: wedding venue, event planner, and performance/event venue.
- Confirmed service language: event planning and management, venue hosting, and customizable stage design.
- The website booking form is an enquiry, not an instant confirmed reservation. Availability and final arrangements require staff confirmation.
- No backend, payment flow, CRM integration, capacity, pricing, catering menu, package list, operating hours, or formal cancellation policy has been supplied.
- Do not invent commercial claims, awards, customer counts, event counts, ratings beyond sourced public evidence, or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the public-facing name “Shafi Complex & Marquee”; “Shafi Marquee” may be used as a compact label where space requires it.
- The public voice may be warm, celebratory, direct, and locally grounded, without generic luxury superlatives or fabricated promises.
- Preserve the authentic venue imagery and recognizable profile identity where image quality permits.
- Avoid generic SaaS structure, purple gradients, pervasive glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, fake statistics, invented testimonials, and generic AI copy.
- The experience must feel premium, distinctive, highly polished, extremely clear, and production-ready on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public Facebook profile with venue name, business categories, address, phone, email, photographs, recent event-stage content, and a TikTok link.
- Independent directory listings corroborate the Jaranwala address, phone number, and event-management category.
- Authentic Facebook profile, cover, venue, and stage photographs are available publicly; usage rights and original high-resolution source files have not been supplied and must be confirmed or replaced by owner-provided originals before commercial launch.
- A formal logo source file, brand guidelines, approved colours, typography, capacity, pricing, packages, catering details, testimonials, booking terms, privacy policy, and final business copy must be supplied later.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the real place and real work, not unsupported claims.
2. Make the route from inspiration to enquiry obvious within seconds.
3. Treat booking as a respectful human handoff with clear expectations.
4. Use authentic event imagery as evidence while keeping essential content readable and accessible.
5. Preserve clarity and confidence at every viewport size.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, descriptive alternative text, and form labels/errors that work with assistive technology.
